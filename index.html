Read before interpreting

A ranking of what YouTube returned — not all of YouTube.

01Not exhaustive. YouTube search returns ranked samples; popular Shorts may be absent.

02Region means market. Japan selects the Japanese market; it does not prove creator nationality.

03Periods describe publication. Results are ranked by current lifetime views, not views gained within the period.

04Velocity is an average. Views divided by age is not recent hourly growth.

05Custom dates are post-filtered. They cannot reveal videos omitted by search.

06Unofficial and breakable. YouTube changes or throttling can interrupt its undocumented interface.
